Start your first paragraph here. You can introduce the reading, explain what stood out to you, and describe your first reaction to the argument or idea.

Use the second paragraph to get more specific. You might connect the reading to design, technology, class themes, or your own experiences and observations.

End with what the reading changed for you, what questions it raised, or what you are still unsure about after finishing it.
